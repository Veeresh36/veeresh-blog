## My Experience Using It Day to Day

The first thing I noticed was how dramatically it simplified packing.

Instead of stuffing chargers into random compartments, I now have one dedicated pouch that goes in and comes out of my bag as a single unit. No hunting. No untangling. Just open and pick.

The organizer opens completely flat — and this matters more than it sounds. Being able to see everything at once instead of digging through layers is the feature I use most.

It has become a permanent part of my daily carry. Whenever I leave home, I grab the organizer and know everything is already packed and in its place.

## What is Good — Pros

### Organisation that actually works

Everything has a designated spot. Chargers, cables, memory cards, adapters, and power banks stay separated and easy to find without rummaging.

### Elastic loops that genuinely prevent tangles

I was sceptical about this before buying. But the loops actually do their job. My USB-C and Lightning cables no longer knot at the bottom of my bag.

### Compact and lightweight

It slides into any backpack without taking up meaningful space or adding weight. You genuinely forget it is there until you need it.

### Perfect for the Indian developer's daily commute

Office, coffee shop, client meeting, weekend trip — it handles all of these without needing to repack or reorganise between uses.

### Durable canvas build

The construction feels solid enough for everyday use. No loose stitching, no weak zips after regular use.

---

## What Could Be Better — Cons

### Not built for large devices

No laptops, no tablets, no bulky camera gear. This is strictly a cables-and-accessories pouch.

### Fills up faster than you expect

Once you pack multiple chargers, a power bank, and accessories, the space fills up quickly. If you carry a heavy tech load, you may need two pouches or a larger case.

### Not waterproof

The canvas provides basic protection but will not survive heavy rain. Keep it inside a bag rather than an outer pocket in wet conditions.

### Limited colour and style options

There is not much choice in terms of design variations if you care about aesthetics beyond the basic canvas look.

### Is the DailyObjects organizer good for travel in India?

Yes. Its compact design makes it ideal for flights, train travel, office commutes, and everyday carry. It fits easily inside most backpacks without taking up meaningful space.

### Will it fit a laptop?

No. This organizer is designed specifically for cables, chargers, and small gadgets. For laptop storage you need a separate sleeve or padded compartment.
